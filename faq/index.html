FAQ

Short answers about how we work.

Every answer here is taken from our home page.

Who is behind this?

Slick Jacket Web Rescue is a small, online-only service. We use AI tools to help with research, writing and building, and a person approves the work and every email we send.

What does the $99 Single fix cover?

A Single fix is $99 flat, paid in advance, for one specific problem on the site you already have: a broken link or form, a typo, wrong hours or contact details, or a layout bug.

My organization has its own web team. Can you still help?

Yes. That's what our $99 look-and-report is for. We check your public pages in desktop and phone browsers for broken images, dead links and buttons, and send one written report with screenshots and exact URLs for your team to fix. No logins, and we don't touch your systems. It's paid in advance and delivered 2 business days after payment. If the report isn't a clear, usable list, we refund the full $99.

Is the free check really free?

Yes. You get three specific suggestions and there's no obligation to hire us.

What happens before any work starts?

We email you exactly what we'll do, the price and the turnaround. Nothing starts until you reply to agree.

When do I pay?

Every job is paid in advance, before the work starts: $99 jobs (a single fix, a look-and-report, an accessibility check or a Profile Fix-Up) and bigger jobs too. We email you a payment request. If you need a different arrangement, ask us and we'll send you a custom quote.

What if you can't fix my problem?

If we can't fix it, you don't pay. If we don't complete the fix we agreed to in writing, there's no charge, and if you've already paid, we refund you in full. Website fixes are paid in advance, and a Single fix is $99 flat.

Do I need my passwords for Google?

We never ask for your passwords. For Google Business Profile work you add us as a Manager through Google's own invite.

How long does it take?

A single fix, fix bundle or accessibility check + top fixes takes 3-5 business days, and a new site about 1-2 weeks, counted from when we have your content. A look-and-report or accessibility check is delivered 2 business days after payment. Turnaround starts once we have your content and your payment.

Do you meet in person or call?

No. Everything happens by email, which also gives you a written record of what we agreed.

If you would like a second pair of eyes, start with our Free 3-point website check, and if one thing needs fixing, a Single fix is $99 flat, paid in advance.
