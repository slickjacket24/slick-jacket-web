FAQ

Short answers about how we work.

Who is behind this?

Slick Jacket Web Rescue is a small, online-only service. We use AI tools to help with research, writing and building, and a person approves the work and every email we send.

What does the $99 Single fix cover?

You get one specific problem fixed on the site you already have. That can be a broken link or form, a typo, wrong hours or contact details, or a layout bug.

The $99 covers a written scope before any work starts and the one fix described in that scope. It is $99 flat, paid in advance.

It takes 3-5 business days, counted from when we have your content and your payment.

This scope does not include any other changes unless you ask for them in writing first.

You stay the owner. Access is by a user or collaborator invite only, and we never ask for your passwords.

If we can't fix it, you don't pay. If we don't complete the fix we agreed to in writing, there's no charge, and if you've already paid, we refund you in full.

My organization has its own web team. Can you still help?

Yes. That's what our $99 look-and-report is for. You get one written report with screenshots and exact URLs of broken images, dead links and buttons we found on your public pages, checked in desktop and phone browsers, for your team to fix.

The $99 covers that check and the written report. It is $99 flat, paid in advance, and is delivered 2 business days after payment.

It does not include making the fixes. No logins, and we don't touch your systems.

If the report isn't a clear, usable list, we refund the full $99.

What does the $99 Accessibility check cover?

We scan the public pages you name (your home page and up to 2 others) for common accessibility problems: missing alt text, contrast, keyboard use, form labels, headings and link text.

You get one written report with what we found and how each could be fixed.

No logins, and we don't touch your systems.

It is $99 flat, paid in advance, and is delivered 2 business days after payment.

If the report isn't a clear, usable list, we refund the full $99.

This is a practical check of common issues, not a legal review, and it is not a claim that the site meets any standard.

What does Accessibility check + top fixes ($199-249) cover?

The accessibility check, plus we fix the top issues on the agreed pages (your home page and up to 2 others), as listed in the written scope: alt text, contrast, keyboard use, form labels, headings and link text.

Paid in advance, before the work starts.

Access is by a user or collaborator invite only, and we never ask for your passwords.

Issues we can't change (for example a locked theme or a third-party widget) are written up with options.

The refund promise below covers the fixes we agreed to in writing, not compliance.

It does not mean the site meets any legal or accessibility standard.

Website fixes are paid in advance. If we can't fix it, you don't pay. If we don't complete the fix we agreed to in writing, there's no charge, and if you've already paid, we refund you in full.

When do I pay?

Every job is paid in advance, before the work starts: $99 jobs (a single fix, a look-and-report, an accessibility check or a Profile Fix-Up) and bigger jobs too. We email you a payment request. If you need a different arrangement, ask us and we'll send you a custom quote.

What if you can't fix my problem?

If we can't fix it, you don't pay. If we don't complete the fix we agreed to in writing, there's no charge, and if you've already paid, we refund you in full. Website fixes are paid in advance, and a Single fix is $99 flat.

What if the same problem comes back?

If a fix we made breaks again from the same cause within 14 days, we'll re-fix it free. Email us and we'll take care of it. It's included in the price you paid, so there's nothing extra to pay.

Do I need my passwords for Google?

We never ask for your passwords. For Google Business Profile work you add us as a Manager through Google's own invite.

How long does it take?

A single fix, fix bundle or accessibility check + top fixes takes 3-5 business days, and a new site about 1-2 weeks, counted from when we have your content. A look-and-report or accessibility check is delivered 2 business days after payment. Turnaround starts once we have your content and your payment.
