A few common questions

What does $99 cover?

The $99 covers a written scope before any work starts and the one fix described in that scope. That can be a broken link or form, a typo, wrong hours or contact details, or a layout bug. We'll explain what's included before you decide.

What if it needs more than $99 of work?

We'll tell you first and explain what we found. You decide whether you want to move forward. Nothing extra happens without your OK.

Do you need my password?

No. We don't need passwords to do the initial check. Access is by a user or collaborator invite only, and we never ask for your passwords.

How long does the fix take?

A single fix, fix bundle or accessibility check + top fixes takes 3-5 business days, and a new site about 1-2 weeks, counted from when we have your content. A look-and-report or accessibility check is delivered 2 business days after payment. Turnaround starts once we have your content and your payment.
