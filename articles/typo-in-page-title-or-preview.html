A typo in your page title or search preview, and how to fix it

When someone opens your site, the first words they may see are not the headline on the page. They may be the short title in the browser tab, or the title and line of text in a Google result or in a preview when the link is shared. A typo there is easy to miss if you only proof the visible page, and it can make a current business look less careful than it is. This is one of the small breaks we keep seeing, and the fix is usually a short edit you can do yourself.

Where the typo hides

The browser tab. Every page has a title that appears in the tab, often set in a separate field from the heading on the page. The tab may still say "Home," or "Welcome to" plus a misspelled name, even when the visible heading is spelled correctly.

The search or share preview. Google, and many apps that show a link preview, often use a separate title and description. In a site editor those fields may be labeled SEO title, meta title, or social title. They can stay wrong after the heading on the page has been fixed.

The first heading on the page. A typo in the large headline at the top of the home page is just as easy for a visitor to notice. Read it out loud. A missing letter is easy to skim past when you already know what the line is supposed to say.

Your Google Business Profile. The name and description on your Google listing are separate from your website. Changing one does not automatically change the other. A typo there shows up in Search and Maps.

How to check, in about five minutes

  1. Open your home page on a computer and look at the browser tab. Then do the same on a couple of other main pages.
  2. Search for your business name in Google and read the blue title and the line of text under it. Check that they match the name you use, spelled the way you want.
  3. Paste your home-page link into a text message to yourself and see what preview appears, if any.
  4. Read the main headline out loud, then the large buttons or menu labels at the top of the page.
  5. Open your Google listing in Search or Maps and read the name and the description out loud.

If the tab, the search title, and the heading do not match, write down each version so you can fix every copy, not only the one you noticed first.

How to fix it yourself

In most site editors, the tab title is in a field labeled something like "Page title," "SEO title," or "meta title." The preview used when a link is shared may be in a nearby field for a social title or a share title. Change every field that still has the typo, not only the heading on the page, then publish the change — saving a draft is not always enough. Check again in a private or incognito window; you may be looking at a saved copy at first. Google and apps that show link previews can take a few days or longer to pick up the new title.

If the typo is in your Google Business Profile, edit the name or description in the profile editor. Google reviews profile edits before they show. Google says this usually takes up to 10 minutes but can sometimes take up to 30 days. Then look at the listing again in Search and in Maps.
