Questions

Who is behind this?

Slick Jacket Web Rescue is a small, online-only service. We use AI tools to help with research, writing and building, and a person approves the work and every email we send.

Do you meet in person or call?

No. Everything happens by email, which also gives you a written record of what we agreed.

Is the free check really free?

Yes. You get three specific suggestions and there's no obligation to hire us.

Can you promise more customers or a higher Google ranking?

No. Nobody honestly can. We promise clear, careful work on what we agreed in writing.

Do I need my passwords for Google?

We never ask for your passwords. For Google Business Profile work you add us as a Manager through Google's own invite.

What if you can't fix my problem?

If we can't fix it, you don't pay. If we don't complete the fix we agreed to in writing, there's no charge, and if you've already paid, we refund you in full. Website fixes are paid in advance, and a Single fix is $99 flat.

How long does it take?

A single fix, fix bundle or accessibility check + top fixes takes 3-5 business days, and a new site about 1-2 weeks, counted from when we have your content. A look-and-report or accessibility check is delivered 2 business days after payment.

I'm not a business. Can you still help?

Yes. We build and fix sites for creators, authors, freelancers, events, nonprofits and personal projects too, as long as the work is legal and honest. Website Care works for any site.

Do you offer a nonprofit discount?

Yes. Registered nonprofits, churches and volunteer groups get 25% off any Website Care plan.

Will you send me follow-up emails?

If we email you first, we send at most two short follow-ups and then we stop. Replying “no thanks” stops them right away. If you ask us for something, we answer. If you'd rather not hear from us, tell us and we'll stop.
