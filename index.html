How it works

  1. Written scope first. We email you exactly what we'll do, the price and the turnaround.
  2. Your written yes. Nothing starts until you reply to agree.
  3. $99 jobs are paid in advance. A $99 single fix or Profile Fix-Up is paid before the work starts. We email you a payment request.
  4. We do the work. Turnaround starts once we have your content and, for a $99 job, your payment.
  5. Review and one round of changes. You see the work and send your changes in one round.
  6. Payment and launch for bigger jobs. We email a payment request when payment is due.

Payment: $99 jobs (a single fix or a Profile Fix-Up) are paid in advance, before the work starts. For bigger jobs, your written scope says when each payment is due, so you know before you say yes. We email you a payment request when payment is due. You can pay by Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, X Money or check.

Questions

Who is behind this?

Slick Jacket Web Rescue is a small, online-only service. We use AI tools to help with research, writing and building, and a person approves the work and every email we send.

Do you meet in person or call?

No. Everything happens by email, which also gives you a written record of what we agreed.

Is the free check really free?

Yes. You get three specific suggestions and there's no obligation to hire us.

Can you promise more customers or a higher Google ranking?

No. Nobody honestly can. We promise clear, careful work on what we agreed in writing.

Do I need my passwords for Google?

We never ask for your passwords. For Google Business Profile work you add us as a Manager through Google's own invite.

What if you can't fix my problem?

Then you don't pay. If we can't complete the fix we agreed to in writing, there's no charge, and if you've already paid, we refund you in full.

How long does it take?

A single fix or fix bundle takes 3-5 business days, and a new site about 1-2 weeks, counted from when we have your content.

I'm not a business. Can you still help?

Yes. We build and fix sites for creators, authors, freelancers, events, nonprofits and personal projects too, as long as the work is legal and honest. Website Care works for any site.

What's the difference between Website Care and Online Care?

Website Care looks after your website itself. Online Care looks after your Google Business Profile, for businesses that have one. You can choose either, both or neither.

Do you offer a nonprofit discount?

Yes. Registered nonprofits, churches and volunteer groups get 25% off any Website Care plan.

Will you keep emailing me?

No. If you ask us for something, we answer. If you'd rather not hear from us, tell us and we'll stop.
